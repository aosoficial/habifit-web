HabiFit Social Scheduler

HabiFit Social Scheduler is an internal social media publishing integration used by the HabiFit team with Postiz. It lets authorized HabiFit-managed TikTok accounts connect through OAuth and publish or schedule HabiFit marketing videos.

Purpose

This integration is not the HabiFit iOS app. It is a social media operations tool used to manage owned TikTok channels from Postiz.

TikTok Data Use

  • OAuth is used to connect TikTok accounts owned or managed by HabiFit.
  • Basic account information is used to identify the connected channel in Postiz.
  • Video upload and publish permissions are used only when our team schedules or publishes HabiFit content.
  • We do not sell TikTok data, scrape TikTok, track users, or allow posting to accounts without OAuth consent.
